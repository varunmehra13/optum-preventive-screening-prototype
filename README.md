# Optum Preventive Screening Experience

A healthcare experience designed to help users move from preventive screening recommendation to confident completion.

Live Prototype: https://optum-preventive-screening-prototype-agv6sa2eb.vercel.app/

---

## Overview

Preventive screenings play an important role in early detection and long-term health management. However, receiving a recommendation does not always translate into action.

Users often hesitate because they lack clarity around:

- Why the screening is recommended
- Whether it is necessary for them
- What the process involves
- How much it will cost
- Where they should complete it
- What happens after booking

This project explores how we can design a clearer path from recommendation → understanding → decision → booking → follow-up.

---

# Problem

A healthcare recommendation alone is not enough to drive completion.

The experience needs to help users make an informed decision by reducing uncertainty and building confidence throughout the journey.

### Key user barriers

**Understanding**
- "Why am I being recommended this screening?"
- "Is this relevant for my health?"

**Cost uncertainty**
- "How much will I have to pay?"
- "Are there unexpected costs?"

**Decision fatigue**
- "Which provider should I choose?"
- "What option works best for me?"

**Preparation anxiety**
- "What do I need to do before the appointment?"
- "What happens after the screening?"

---

# Goal

Design an experience that increases preventive screening completion by making the journey easier to understand and act on.

The experience focuses on:

- Providing context before asking users to take action
- Making healthcare decisions transparent
- Reducing uncertainty during provider selection
- Supporting users beyond appointment booking

---

# Design Approach

## 1. Explain before asking users to act

Instead of immediately pushing users toward booking, the experience first establishes:

- Why the screening matters
- What the recommendation means
- What users can expect

---

## 2. Reduce decision complexity

The experience helps users confidently choose by surfacing:

- Relevant screening information
- Provider options
- Cost expectations
- Appointment availability

---

## 3. Continue support after conversion

The healthcare journey does not end after booking.

The experience provides:

- Appointment confirmation
- Preparation guidance
- Follow-up information
- Clear next steps
